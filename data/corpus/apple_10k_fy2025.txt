Apple Inc. — 10-K Annual Report, Fiscal Year 2025 (ended September 27, 2025)
Source: SEC Filing, Accession No. 0000320193-25-000079, Filed October 31, 2025

CONSOLIDATED STATEMENTS OF INCOME

Full Fiscal Year 2025:
Total revenue: $416 billion.
Annual EPS growth: double-digit year-over-year.

First Quarter Fiscal 2025 (Q1 FY25, ended December 28, 2024):
Revenue: $124.3 billion, up 4% year-over-year.
Diluted EPS: $2.40, up 10% YoY.
All-time records for total company revenue and EPS.
Services revenue reached a new all-time high.
Cash returned to shareholders: over $30 billion during the quarter.
Quarterly dividend: $0.25 per share.

Third Quarter Fiscal 2025 (Q3 FY25, ended June 28, 2025):
Revenue: $94.0 billion, up 10% year-over-year.
Diluted EPS: $1.57, up 12% YoY.
June quarter records for total company revenue, iPhone revenue, and EPS.
Services revenue reached a new all-time high.
Double-digit growth in iPhone, Mac, and Services.
Quarterly dividend: $0.26 per share.

Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2025 (Q4 FY25, ended September 27, 2025):
Revenue: $102.5 billion, up 8% year-over-year.
Diluted EPS: $1.85 (adjusted), up 13% YoY.

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE (FY2025):
iPhone revenue: September quarter record achieved (Q4), June quarter record achieved (Q3).
Services revenue: all-time high reached in Q1, Q3, and Q4.
Mac revenue: double-digit growth in Q3.
Installed base of active devices: new all-time high across all product categories and all geographic segments.

SHAREHOLDER RETURNS:
Q4 FY2025 quarterly dividend: $0.26 per share, payable November 13, 2025.
Q3 FY2025 quarterly dividend: $0.26 per share.
Q1 FY2025 quarterly dividend: $0.25 per share.
Q1 FY2025 cash returned to shareholders: over $30 billion.

Note: Apple does not publicly break out individual segment revenue dollar figures in press releases. Detailed segment revenue data (iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables/Home/Accessories, Services) is available in the 10-K filing's financial statements.
