Apple Inc. — 10-K Annual Report, Fiscal Year 2025 (ended September 27, 2025)
Source: SEC Filing, Accession No. 0000320193-25-000079, Filed October 31, 2025

CONSOLIDATED STATEMENTS OF INCOME

Full Fiscal Year 2025:
Total revenue: $416 billion.
Diluted earnings per share: $1.85 (adjusted, Q4 FY2025).
EPS growth: 13% increase YoY (adjusted) in Q4.
Annual EPS growth: double-digit.

Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2025 (Q4 FY25, ended September 27, 2025):
Revenue: $102.5 billion, up 8% year-over-year.
Diluted EPS: $1.85 (adjusted).

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE:
iPhone revenue: September quarter record achieved.
Services revenue: all-time high reached.
Installed base of active devices: new all-time high across all product categories and all geographic segments.

SHAREHOLDER RETURNS:
Quarterly dividend: $0.26 per share, payable November 13, 2025.

Note: Apple does not publicly break out individual segment revenue figures (Mac, iPad, Wearables, Home and Accessories) in press releases. Detailed segment data is available in the 10-K filing's financial statements.
