SEC Marketing Rule — Rule 206(4)-1 Under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940
Source: SEC.gov, as amended through 2024

OVERVIEW:
The SEC's marketing rule (Rule 206(4)-1) governs advertisements by investment advisers registered with the SEC. The amended rule became effective November 4, 2022.

KEY REQUIREMENTS:
1. All advertisements must be fair and balanced, presenting material risks alongside potential returns.
2. Hypothetical performance presentations must include all relevant assumptions, risks, and limitations.
3. Third-party ratings must disclose the methodology used and any compensation paid to the rating provider.
4. Testimonials and endorsements require clear and prominent disclosure of material conflicts of interest.
5. Net performance must accompany any presentation of gross performance, with equal prominence.

RECORD-KEEPING:
Advisers must retain copies of all advertisements and related records for a minimum of 5 years from the date of last use.

COMPLIANCE REVIEW:
All marketing materials must be reviewed and approved by a designated compliance officer prior to distribution.

ENFORCEMENT:
The SEC examined 312 investment advisers for marketing rule compliance in 2024.
Deficiencies were found in 28% of examined firms.
Penalties range from warning letters for minor first-time infractions to fines up to $250,000 per violation for material misrepresentations.
Repeated or egregious violations can result in registration revocation.
